Settings (**Configurações**) open from the account menu at the top right, or with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>,</kbd>. Each section handles one part of the app:

<Steps>

1. **Geral** (General): how much to keep offline, each account's sync, and the email archives opened on this computer.

2. **Aparência** (Appearance): theme, background color and list density.

3. **Notificações** (Notifications): what a notification shows, quiet hours, which accounts notify and starting with Windows.

4. **Privacidade** (Privacy): EpistulaMail password, automatic lock, keeping less (erasing what is outside the period) and rotating the database key.

5. **Regras** (Rules): each account's rules, their order and the history of what they did.

6. **Criptografia** (Encryption): OpenPGP keys, S/MIME certificates and what happens when replying to encrypted messages.

</Steps>

Each section has its own page in the **Documentation**, with the details.
