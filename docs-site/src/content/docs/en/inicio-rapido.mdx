Install EpistulaMail, connect your account and read your first email in about five minutes. By the end you will have your inbox synced and stored on your computer in an encrypted database.

## What you need

- **Windows 10 or 11, or Linux**, 64-bit.
- **A Gmail, Microsoft or manual (Other) account.** Yahoo, iCloud, Zoho, GMX and AOL are coming soon; meanwhile you can [open email archives](/en/guia/arquivos/abrir-um-arquivo/) with no account.
- **On Linux, the Secret Service**: GNOME Keyring or KWallet. It is the system vault where the database key and account tokens live.

## Step by step

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Windows">

<Steps>

1. **Download the installer.** On [epistulamail.com](https://www.epistulamail.com), click "Download EpistulaMail" and choose Windows. The file is about 11 MB.

2. **Check the file.** In PowerShell, in the download folder:

   ```powershell
   (Get-FileHash .\EpistulaMail_1.1.0_x64-setup.exe).Hash
   ```

   The result must match the SHA-256 published in the [release notes](https://github.com/elyosemite/epistulamail/releases/latest).

3. **Install.** The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows shows "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info** and then **Run anyway**. It installs for your user only, with no administrator rights.

4. **Connect your account.** Open EpistulaMail and choose Gmail, Outlook or [Other](/en/guia/contas/). Sign-in happens on your provider's page, in your browser; your password never goes through the app.

5. **Read and search.** The most recent emails show up first and the rest arrives in the background. Press <kbd>/</kbd> and type `de: Ana tem:anexo` to try the chip search.

</Steps>

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Linux">

<Steps>

1. **Download the package.** On [epistulamail.com](https://www.epistulamail.com), click "Download EpistulaMail" and choose Linux. The AppImage is a single file; the `.deb` is for Debian and Ubuntu.

2. **Check the file.** In a terminal, in the download folder:

   ```bash
   sha256sum EpistulaMail_1.1.0_amd64.AppImage
   ```

   The result must match the SHA-256 published in the [release notes](https://github.com/elyosemite/epistulamail/releases/latest).

3. **Install.** For the AppImage:

   ```bash
   chmod +x EpistulaMail_1.1.0_amd64.AppImage
   ./EpistulaMail_1.1.0_amd64.AppImage
   ```

   For the `.deb`:

   ```bash
   sudo apt install ./EpistulaMail_1.1.0_amd64.deb
   ```

   The app needs the Secret Service (GNOME Keyring or KWallet) to open. If it is not available, EpistulaMail shows a message and does not touch your data.

4. **Connect your account.** Open EpistulaMail and choose Gmail, Outlook or [Other](/en/guia/contas/). Sign-in happens on your provider's page, in your browser; your password never goes through the app.

5. **Read and search.** The most recent emails show up first and the rest arrives in the background. Press <kbd>/</kbd> and type `de: Ana tem:anexo` to try the chip search.

</Steps>
