To encrypt for someone, you need their public key. It can arrive:

- **attached to an email** or through the **Autocrypt** header: it comes in as "not yet accepted";
- **from a file** or the clipboard;
- **from the internet**, only when you ask: EpistulaMail asks the person's domain and `keys.openpgp.org`.

<AppPreview name="keys" />

Acceptance follows Thunderbird's style: not yet, accepted, verified or rejected.
