Uninstall it from Windows, under **Settings → Apps → EpistulaMail**.

The uninstaller removes the program. To also erase the data:

1. Delete the `%APPDATA%\br.com.epistula.desktop` folder.
2. In **Credential Manager → Windows Credentials**, remove the entries of the `epistula` service.
3. If you want, remove EpistulaMail's access at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Your messages stay on your email server.
