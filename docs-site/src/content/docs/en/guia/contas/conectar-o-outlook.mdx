When you choose Outlook, EpistulaMail opens Microsoft's sign-in page in your browser. You sign in, authorize and come back to the app. It works for Outlook.com, Hotmail and Microsoft 365.

EpistulaMail asks only for:

- **`https://graph.microsoft.com/Mail.ReadWrite`**: read and organize your email through Microsoft Graph.
- **`https://graph.microsoft.com/Mail.Send`**: send email through Microsoft Graph.
- **`openid`, `email` and `offline_access`**: your address and name, to show the account, and permission to keep the connection without asking for sign-in on every sync.

It does not ask for contacts, calendar, files, OneDrive or a profile beyond the address and name. Your Microsoft password never goes through EpistulaMail.

<Aside type="note" title="Work or school accounts">
In Microsoft 365, the tenant administrator may need to grant consent for the Microsoft Graph permissions. If the connection is refused, ask your administrator.
</Aside>

The refresh token lives in the system vault (Windows Credential Manager, Linux Secret Service), with the same care as Google accounts: never in the database in plain text, never in a file, never in a log. To revoke access, use [account.live.com/consent/Manage](https://account.live.com/consent/Manage) or the Microsoft 365 consent page.
