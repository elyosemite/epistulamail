When you choose Gmail, EpistulaMail opens Google's sign-in page in your browser. You sign in there, authorize, and go back to the app.

EpistulaMail asks for:

- **`https://mail.google.com/`**: read, send and organize your emails over IMAP and SMTP. It is the only Google permission that grants access to those protocols.
- **`openid`, `email` and `profile`**: your address, name and photo, to show the account.

Details in [How EpistulaMail uses your Google account](https://www.epistulamail.com/conta-google) (in Portuguese).

For personal Outlook.com, Hotmail and Live accounts, see [Connect Outlook](/en/guia/contas/conectar-o-outlook/).
