After each sync, the messages in the period chosen in **Configurações → Geral** (Settings → General) are downloaded in the background, without marking them as read: *Últimos 30 dias* (last 30 days), *Últimos 90 dias* (last 90 days), *Tudo* (everything) or *Só ao abrir* (only when opened).

Without internet, the inbox, the reader and the search keep working with what is stored.
