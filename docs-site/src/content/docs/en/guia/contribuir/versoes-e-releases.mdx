1. `./scripts/release.ps1 <version>` bumps the version in the files and opens the section in `CHANGELOG.md`.
2. Commit, push and the `v<version>` tag.
3. The pipeline builds, signs the update and creates a draft release.
4. Once the release is published, the website and the bell notice follow on their own.
