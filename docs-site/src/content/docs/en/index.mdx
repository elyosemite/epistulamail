## Where to start

If this is your first time, follow the **Quickstart**: it installs EpistulaMail, connects your account and shows the search. If you already use it, the **Documentation** covers each feature in detail, with what happens behind the scenes and where your data lives.

Everything here applies to Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). For now, only Gmail accounts can connect; other providers are coming soon. Opening old email archives works with no account at all.

## What EpistulaMail is

- **Yours, with no catch.** There is no EpistulaMail account, no EpistulaMail cloud and no telemetry. No email goes through a server of ours.
- **Encrypted on disk.** Messages live in a database encrypted with AES-256, with the key in the Windows Credential Manager.
- **Works offline.** What has been downloaded opens and can be searched without internet.
- **Open source.** Everything is on [GitHub](https://github.com/elyosemite/epistulamail).
